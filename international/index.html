Roadmap: from idea to operating FL LLC

A typical cross-border setup has 7 moving parts. Here's the order most clients follow. Wilton Plaza fits into step 3 — the rest you handle with specialized providers.

  1. Florida Registered Agent. Required by law for every FL LLC (FL Statute 605.0113). Typical cost $99–299/year. Options include our application-only RA service, LegalZoom, Northwest RA, Incfile. Must be arranged before or at the time of filing the LLC.
  2. File LLC with Sunbiz. Florida Division of Corporations. DIY at sunbiz.org for about $125, or through a formation service. You will need a chosen business name, RA designation, and member/manager info.
  3. Get your Wilton Plaza address. Sign up for Business Address ($39/mo) or Virtual Office ($49/mo). Use the assigned suite as principal office and mailing address on your Sunbiz filing or amendment.
  4. EIN (Federal Tax ID). Non-US residents without SSN/ITIN must apply via IRS Form SS-4 by fax (1-855-215-1627 from abroad) or by mail to IRS Austin, TX. Typically 4–6 weeks by fax. The IRS online portal is SSN/ITIN-only. Some formation services offer EIN-obtain as an add-on.
  5. Annual IRS filing — Form 5472. Single-member foreign-owned LLCs must file Form 5472 + pro-forma Form 1120 every year to report owner information. Missed filing = $25,000+ per-year penalty. Plan on hiring a US CPA familiar with foreign-owned LLCs.
  6. Open a US business bank account. Online-first for non-US: Mercury, Relay, Wise Business. Branch-visit typically required: Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo. Each bank sets its own acceptance rules — confirm with your bank before starting.
  7. Ongoing compliance. Sunbiz annual report ($138.75/year, due by May 1). Federal tax filing (Form 5472 or other depending on structure). Any state nexus obligations if operating in other US states.

Wilton Plaza handles step 3. For the rest, you will need specialists (CPA, attorney, RA, bank) — we can point you to resources, but we are not those providers.

Honest about what we don't do

Cross-border setups break when someone overpromises. Here's the boundary so you know before you pay.

  • LLC formation. We provide an address you can use on a Florida LLC filing. We do not form LLCs for you. Use Sunbiz directly, or a US-based formation service, or a cross-border attorney.
  • EIN / IRS tax ID. Applying for an EIN as a non-US resident requires a specific IRS process (typically Form SS-4 by fax or mail for non-US applicants). We do not apply for EINs on your behalf.
  • Tax advice. We are an address and office services provider, not tax advisors. Cross-border tax — US LLC with foreign owner, withholding, double taxation treaties, state nexus — requires a qualified CPA or tax attorney.
  • Bank account opening. US banks decide their own acceptance rules. Our address is commonly accepted, but many US banks require you to visit a branch in person to open an account. Online-first banks (Mercury, Relay) may work fully remote depending on your entity structure.
  • Immigration / visa. We do not provide any immigration, visa, or residency services. A business address in Florida does not confer residency or legal presence.
  • Sanctioned countries. We screen applications against the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals list and cannot accept clients from sanctioned jurisdictions. Denied applications receive a full refund (Terms §3.7, §4.5). If you are unsure whether your location triggers a sanctions issue, call us before purchasing.
