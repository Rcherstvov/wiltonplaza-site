After Activation: How to Use Your Address

Your service is live. Here's how to get the most out of it — Sunbiz updates, scan requests, pickups, conference room, notary, and contact.

Received your Activation Notice? Great — that's our email confirming your suite number is assigned and you may begin using the Wilton Plaza address on business filings, licenses, websites, and documents. This guide walks you through the six things most clients do in their first month.

Haven't received your Activation Notice yet? Do NOT use the address until you do (Terms §4.1). Payment does not equal activation. If it's been more than 3 business days, call (954) 249-5388.

1

Update Sunbiz with your new address

This is the most common first task. If you have an existing Florida LLC or corporation, update the principal office address and mailing address to your Wilton Plaza suite.

  1. Go to sunbiz.org and sign in to file an amendment.
  2. Choose the amendment form for your entity type (LLC Articles of Amendment, Corporate Articles of Amendment, etc.).
  3. Update Principal Office Address and Mailing Address to: 1881 NE 26th Street, Suite [your number], Wilton Manors, FL 33305.
  4. Submit the amendment and pay the Sunbiz filing fee (typically $25 for LLC amendments, $35 for corporations).
  5. The change takes effect immediately in Sunbiz records.

Registered Agent is separate. Updating the Principal Office Address on Sunbiz does NOT update your Registered Agent. If you need to change or designate a Registered Agent, that is a separate amendment. See Registered Agent FAQ.

2

Request a mail scan

Scan requests are made by email. We open and scan the specific item you ask for and email the PDF back to you.

  • Email virtualoffice@megamgmt.net during business hours (Mon–Fri 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST).
  • Include enough detail to identify the item: sender name, tracking number if known, or "open the envelope from [sender] received [date]".
  • We scan within the same business day when received before 3:00 PM; otherwise next business day.
  • Delivered as PDF attachment to the email address on your account.

Scan allowances: Virtual Office includes up to 20 scan requests per month at no charge. Business Address includes 5. Additional scans are $2 each. See Terms §6.3.

3

Pick up mail or packages in person

Come to the building during business hours with government-issued photo ID.

  • Address: 1881 NE 26th Street, Suite 212, Wilton Manors, FL 33305. Our VO reception is Suite 212.
  • Bring: driver's license, passport, or state ID.
  • Parking: free private lot on-site.
  • Authorizing someone else? Email us written authorization in advance with their full name and ID type. We will not release items to unauthorized persons.

Hold periods: Mail held up to 30 days after notice of availability (Terms §6.2). Packages held up to 30 days with a $15 late-pickup fee if not collected within 7 days. Items beyond 45 days may be disposed as abandoned. Request forwarding before items expire.

If you're outside the US: In-person pickup is not your path. Request forwarding by email when mail arrives — you pay actual postage + $5 handling per shipment. We can also consolidate items into a single shipment to reduce cost. See the International Clients page.

4

Book the conference room

Virtual Office clients: included at no charge. Business Address and walk-ins: $25/hour.

  • Book by email or phone at least 24 hours in advance.
  • VO allowance: 2 sessions per month, up to 2 hours each, maximum 6 persons.
  • Included amenities: Wi-Fi, whiteboard, power outlets, conference table.
  • Cancellation less than 24 hours before start time counts as a used session (Terms §7.1).

Need more than 2 sessions/month? Additional bookings available at $25/hour for VO clients. Business Address clients: $25/hour, 1-hour minimum.

5

Use the on-site notary

Tricia Frost is our licensed Florida notary, on-site Mon–Fri during business hours. Virtual Office clients: free. Business Address and walk-ins: $10/signature (Florida statutory rate).

  • Walk in during business hours — for most documents no appointment is needed.
  • Bring: all signing parties, their government-issued photo ID, and the unsigned document.
  • Good for: affidavits, powers of attorney, contracts, corporate resolutions, loan documents, probate.
  • We do not notarize: real estate closing documents (deeds, mortgages), immigration documents, or vital records (birth/death certificates).
  • For large document packages or multi-party signings, call ahead at (954) 249-5388 to guarantee availability.

Remote Online Notary (RON) available by appointment at $25/signature.

If you're outside the US: On-site notary is Tricia in Wilton Manors — not accessible remotely. For remote document signings, RON at $25/signature is the only option. Book by email, we coordinate a video appointment during Florida business hours. Your country's law may or may not accept US RON — verify before relying on it for foreign filings.

6

Know who to contact

  • Day-to-day (scans, pickup, forwarding, bookings): virtualoffice@megamgmt.net or call (954) 249-5388.
  • Billing or plan changes: email above; include your plan name and the change you need.
  • Urgent (legal mail, time-sensitive package): call (954) 249-5388 during business hours.
  • Off hours: email and we respond next business day.

Business hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST. Closed on federal holidays.

If you're outside the US: All times are US Eastern (EST, UTC-5 standard / UTC-4 daylight). Email is best for time-zone flexibility — we reply within 1 business day. For phone calls, target 9 AM – 5 PM EST which is: 06:00–14:00 Pacific, 14:00–22:00 UK, 15:00–23:00 Madrid, 10:00–18:00 São Paulo, 08:00–16:00 Mexico City.
