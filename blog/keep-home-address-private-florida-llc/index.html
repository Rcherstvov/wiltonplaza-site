Why a Sunbiz Home Address Becomes Public

The Florida Division of Corporations' LLC filing page says submitted information becomes public and available to view. Entity searches display current details and filed documents.

Plan before filing. Replacing a current address does not delete the original document, and other agencies or data providers may retain information independently.

Four Address Areas to Review Before Filing

The headline fields matter, but they are not the whole record. The official Florida LLC instructions and annual-report instructions treat these entries differently:

Principal Office Address

The principal-office field asks for the LLC's main business street address. A business address in Wilton Manors may fit when the agreement authorizes that use and it accurately represents the company. A commercial format alone is not enough.

An address license does not create dedicated premises or satisfy every permit. Confirm public-access, records, signage, inspection, and licensed-premises rules separately.

Mailing Address

The mailing address is for correspondence when different. Sunbiz allows a P.O. Box, or the same authorized commercial address may fit both fields when accurate.

Registered Agent and Registered Office

The registered agent accepts service of process at a Florida street address. Privacy planning must never involve hiding from legal process, using an agent without consent, or entering an unsuitable address.

Wilton Plaza address plans do not include registered-agent service. Any such service is application-only and a separate engagement, requiring explicit acceptance. Do not make a Wilton Plaza designation without written acceptance.

Manager and Other Principal Details

A home address may reappear beside a manager. Formation manager entries are optional; annual reports require at least one principal and a street address. Review the whole form.

Do not omit required information or use an unrelated location. A licensed address must be authorized and accurate. Ask a Florida attorney about safety or disclosure concerns.

How a Business Address License Helps

A signed license authorizes stated commercial-address uses. For an eligible business, it can provide a consistent address for permitted Sunbiz fields and business materials.

Confirm the entity name, filing use, activation date, recipients, pickup process, and term. Keep the signed agreement and wait for activation.

The license does not change where work occurs. The IRS separately asks about mailing, street, and primary locations. Review the EIN address fields rather than copying a Sunbiz address everywhere.

What an Address Plan Cannot Promise

  • No total anonymity. Florida filings require business and principal information, and other public records may identify an owner.
  • No erasure of prior documents. Updating current details does not guarantee earlier document images or third-party copies disappear.
  • No avoidance of legal notice. The registered-agent arrangement must remain valid and available for service of process.
  • No automatic registered-agent appointment. That role is application-only, separate, and effective only after explicit acceptance.
  • No operational premises by implication. An address license is not a private office, lease, or proof of daily occupancy.
  • No universal agency acceptance. Banks decide what address evidence they accept, and other agencies apply their own rules.

Privacy Checklist for a New Florida LLC

  1. Inventory the public fields. Review the principal-office, mailing, registered-agent, and manager or principal sections.
  2. Choose an accurate principal office. Confirm the address fits the LLC's facts and authorized uses.
  3. Activate before filing. Never submit an address for a service you only plan to buy later.
  4. Plan for pickup. Assign collection and deadline tracking.
  5. Handle the registered agent separately. Select a qualified agent, obtain consent, and enter the accepted Florida registered office.
  6. Review principal addresses. Check each manager or authorized-person entry.
  7. Check other requirements. Review licensing, tax, insurance, and banking rules.
  8. Save the record. Retain the filing, address agreement, and agent acceptance.

Activate the new address and follow current instructions. Earlier documents can remain viewable. Consult a Florida attorney about safety concerns or statutory exemptions; an address plan does not create eligibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my Florida LLC address public on Sunbiz?

Yes. The Division of Corporations says submitted LLC information becomes part of the public record and is available for public view. Review every address field before filing.

Can I use a business address instead of my home address for a Florida LLC?

A licensed business address may fit a permitted field when its use is authorized and accurate. It does not fit every business, filing, license, or field automatically.

What is the difference between a principal address and a mailing address on Sunbiz?

The principal address is the LLC’s principal-office street address. The mailing address is for correspondence when different, and Sunbiz permits a P.O. Box there.

Does a Wilton Plaza business-address plan include registered-agent service?

No. Registered-agent service is application-only, requires a separate engagement, and must be explicitly accepted before designation. A Business Address or Virtual Office plan never authorizes the appointment.

Can I remove a home address after it has appeared on Sunbiz?

You can update current address information through the appropriate Sunbiz process, but filed documents may remain viewable. Ask a Florida attorney about your circumstances or an exemption you may qualify for.

The Bottom Line

Home address privacy for a Florida LLC begins before filing. Use an authorized principal address where it fits, plan for timely in-person pickup, appoint the agent separately, and review principal details. Never avoid legal process or submit false information.
