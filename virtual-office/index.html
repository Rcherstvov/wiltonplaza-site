What Is a Virtual Office?

A virtual office gives your business a real commercial address without the cost of renting physical space. You get mail handling, a professional suite number, and access to office services — but you work from wherever you want.

This is not a P.O. Box. This is not a coworking membership. This is a legitimate business address at a real commercial building, staffed by a real person, five days a week.

At Wilton Plaza, your virtual office includes things most providers charge extra for or don't offer at all: mail scanning included with your plan, a licensed on-site notary at no charge, conference room included, package receiving from every carrier, and private parking when you visit.

Wilton Plaza serves businesses across South Florida, from the Fort Lauderdale and Miami metro area to Palm Beach County.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is included in the $49/month plan?
Everything listed on this page: professional business address with unique suite number, mail scanning included with same-day email delivery, on-site licensed notary at no charge, conference room included, package receiving from all carriers, junk mail filtering, mail forwarding (postage at cost plus $5.00 handling fee per shipment), sign on building directory, FREC compliant address, free private parking, and bilingual service in English and Spanish.
Is the notary really free? What's the catch?
No catch. Tricia Frost is our office administrator and a Florida-licensed notary. She's here every business day. Virtual Office clients pay $0 for notary services — it's included in your plan. Walk-in clients pay $10/signature. We can offer this because Tricia is already on staff — there's no incremental cost to us, so we pass that savings to VO clients.
How does mail scanning work?
Mail scanning is included with your Virtual Office plan. Submit a request by email anytime during business hours. Virtual Office includes 20 scan requests a month — most clients use 4-6. Need more? Just ask. We scan in high resolution and email you a PDF the same day.
Can I register my Florida LLC at this virtual office address?
Yes. You can use this address as your principal office and mailing address on Florida filings. For registered agent service, you can apply for our $99/year Registered Agent plan — accepted by application and subject to eligibility review. Thousands of Florida LLCs use virtual office addresses for state registration.
Does this virtual office meet FREC 475.22 requirements?
Yes. The address meets all requirements under Florida Statute 475.22 for a brokerage office: commercial building, physical sign, staffed during business hours, dedicated suite number. We already serve licensed brokers from this location.
What if I need to meet a client? Can I use the office?
Yes. The conference room is available to Virtual Office clients at no charge. It seats 6, has Wi-Fi, a whiteboard, and power outlets. Walk in during business hours or book ahead through Calendly to guarantee availability.
What carriers do you accept packages from?
All of them. USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, Amazon, OnTrac, LaserShip, and any private courier. If it's addressed to your suite number, we accept it, log it, and notify you by email with a photo.
How do I get started?
Click "Get Your Virtual Office" on this page. Pay $49 through Stripe. You'll receive a welcome email with your suite number. Complete a short onboarding form and provide one government-issued ID — Tricia will notarize on-site for free. Most clients are fully set up within 1-3 business days after verification, and you can begin using the address once you receive your Activation Notice.
How does cancellation work?
All plans carry a 12-month commitment. Cancelling a renewal requires 30 days written notice; early termination of quarterly plans is subject to an early-termination fee — see Terms §3.6. We'll hold your mail for 30 days after cancellation, then return to sender.
What happens to my mail if I'm traveling or out of town?
Email us which envelope you need and Tricia scans it and emails you the PDF (20 scan requests a month included). If you need physical originals forwarded to another address, request a forward by email — we ship at postage cost plus a $5.00 handling fee per shipment. Your office doesn't stop working when you leave town.
