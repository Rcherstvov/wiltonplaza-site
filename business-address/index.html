A Florida Address That Works Statewide — Even If You're Not in Florida

You do not have to live near Wilton Manors — or in Florida at all — to use this address. When you form a Florida LLC or corporation, the Division of Corporations requires a physical street address for your principal office, on your Articles of Organization and on every annual report after that. P.O. Boxes are not accepted. UPS Store mailboxes look like street addresses ("123 Main St #456") but are increasingly flagged by banks, vendors, and government agencies as mailbox services rather than business offices.

Our address is a real commercial building — the kind verification systems treat as an office, not a mailbox. That makes it a clean fit for:

  • Out-of-state companies registering a Florida LLC and establishing a Florida presence
  • Real estate investors managing Florida properties from another state
  • E-commerce sellers who need a physical Florida address for vendor accounts and marketplace verification
  • Anyone whose business operates statewide but needs one compliant principal address on file

Everything is handled remotely: sign up online, complete onboarding, and we receive your mail and forward it on request wherever you are. The address is also FREC compliant for Florida real estate licensing.
