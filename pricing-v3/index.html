Billing & commitment questions

Can I cancel? What notice do I need to give?

All plans have a 12-month term and renew automatically — cancel any renewal with 30-day written notice. We send a renewal reminder by email at least 30 days before each renewal. Cancel early on quarterly billing — you never owe more than 2 months (Terms §3.6). Annual plans: no added early-termination fee.

What exactly is charged on quarterly and annual billing?

Quarterly plans are billed in advance every 3 months ($117 Business Address, $147 Virtual Office). Annual plans are billed once per year at a discount. No setup fees. No deposits.

Are there any setup fees or deposits?

No. There are no setup fees, no activation charges, and no security deposits. Optional charges apply only if you use them (mail forwarding $5 handling + postage, late package pickup $15, and similar — full schedule in Terms §2.3).

Can I use this address for my LLC and Sunbiz filings?

Yes. You may use our address as a public business presence address on Sunbiz filings, business licenses, business cards, websites, and marketing materials. One exception: you may not list us in the Registered Agent field — that requires a separate signed agreement (see the Registered Agent add-on above).

How fast is my address active after payment?

After checkout, you'll receive setup instructions by email within 1 business day. Activation typically completes within 1-3 business days (never more than 5). Your service is active once you receive the Activation Notice by email.

What happens to my mail and packages?

We receive mail and packages during business hours and hold them securely for in-person pickup. Mail forwarding is available on request at postage cost plus a $5 handling fee.

Is there an application or identity check? What if I'm not approved?

We screen for identity, international compliance (sanctions lists), and prohibited uses — no residential use, no drop-shipping, no high-volume mail (Terms §5.2). If we decline during onboarding, you get a full refund (Terms §3.7, §4.5). If a third party (your bank, Google Business Profile, a licensing board) does not accept our address, we can't refund — that's outside our control (Terms §5.4). Unsure your use fits?
