Frequently Asked Questions

What is a business address at Wilton Plaza?
A professional business address gives you a real commercial address in Wilton Manors that you can use on your website, business cards, filings, and marketing. We receive your mail and packages during business hours and hold them for in-person pickup. Virtual Office clients also get conference room access and on-site notary at no additional charge. This is not a registered agent, mail agent, or service-of-process address.
Can I use this address for Florida business filings?
Yes — use it on Sunbiz, licenses, your website, and business cards. The one thing it isn't: a registered agent / legal-notice address. Need that too? Apply for our $99/year Registered Agent service.
How do mail and package receiving work?
We receive mail and packages during business hours and hold them securely for in-person pickup. Mail forwarding is available on request at postage cost plus a $5 handling fee.
Do you offer Registered Agent service?
Registered Agent is a separate $99/year add-on — we personally approve each application. Apply here.
Can I pick up mail in person?
Yes. Come by during business hours (Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST) and pick up any items we are holding for you. Bring a government-issued photo ID. Want someone else to pick up for you? Email us their name ahead of time.
Where is Wilton Plaza located?
Wilton Plaza is located at 1881 NE 26th Street in Wilton Manors, Broward County, South Florida. We are right off I-95 (exit Oakland Park Blvd), 15 minutes from Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL), and 30 minutes from downtown Miami. Wilton Manors is an independent city surrounded by Fort Lauderdale, in the heart of the Fort Lauderdale / Miami metro area.
