Why We Can Offer the Lowest Price

The math is straightforward. Most virtual office providers lease their space. They pay rent, CAM charges, and build-out costs, all of which get passed to clients. When the lease renews at a higher rate, client prices go up. When the building sells to a new owner, the provider may be forced to relocate, and every client has to update their LLC filings, bank accounts, and business licenses.

Wilton Plaza is privately owned and managed, established in 1973. There is no rent to pay, no lease to expire, and no landlord to negotiate with. Our overhead is structurally lower than every leased-space provider in Broward County, and that lower cost translates directly into lower prices for our clients. And because the building is privately owned, your business address is permanent.
