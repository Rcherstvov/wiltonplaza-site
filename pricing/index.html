When another service fits better

Wilton Plaza is great for many things, but a few setups are served better by a different tool. We'd rather point you to the right solution than take your money and disappoint you.

  • High-volume packages or drop-shipping. We're set up for business mail and occasional packages. For regular shipping flow, a fulfillment warehouse or 3PL service is built for exactly that.
  • Live receptionist answering calls in your company name. We handle mail and office facilities, not call answering. Services like Ruby Receptionists or AnswerConnect specialize in this and pair well with a virtual office.
  • Automatic weekly mail forwarding. We forward on request, not on a fixed schedule. If you want mail batched and shipped every week without asking, a mailbox service with scheduled forwarding is a better match.
  • A desk to work from every day. This is a virtual office — but if you want physical office space in the same building, Wilton Plaza has long-term office suites starting from $700/month. Ask about availability or call (954) 249-5388.
  • Residential, vehicle, voter, or homestead registrations. Those require a residential address by law. We're a commercial building, so the right solution is a real home address.

Unsure? Call (954) 249-5388 — we'd rather spend 5 minutes pointing you to the right solution than have you sign up for the wrong one.
