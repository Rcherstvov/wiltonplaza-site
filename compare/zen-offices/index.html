Detailed Comparison

Both Wilton Plaza and ZEN Offices serve the Broward County market with virtual office solutions. Each has a different approach to pricing, features, and service delivery. This comparison breaks down the key differences to help you make an informed decision.

Pricing: $49/month vs. $79/month

The most immediate difference is the monthly price. Wilton Plaza's Virtual Office plan costs $49 per month with no setup fees, no deposits, and quarterly or annual billing. ZEN Offices' comparable plan starts at $79 per month. Over a full year, that is a difference of at least $360.

But the sticker price is only part of the cost equation. At Wilton Plaza, $49 per month includes mail and package receiving with items held for pickup, on-site notary at no extra charge (during business hours, subject to availability), conference room access by appointment (two sessions per month), a sign on the building directory, and private parking included. These services are all bundled into the single monthly price with no add-ons or per-use fees.

ZEN Offices may charge separately for some services that Wilton Plaza includes. When comparing, ask about conference room hourly rates, package handling fees, and any additional charges that are not included in the base monthly rate.

Notary Service: Included vs. Not Available

This is one of the clearest differentiators between the two providers. Wilton Plaza has a licensed notary, Tricia Frost, available during business hours, subject to availability. For Virtual Office clients, notary service is included at no additional cost. Walk-in notary available ($10/signature).

ZEN Offices does not offer on-site notary service as part of their virtual office plans. If you need documents notarized, you will need to find a separate notary public, which typically means a mobile notary at $25 to $75 per visit plus per-signature fees, or a trip to a bank or UPS Store location.

For businesses that handle legal documents regularly, this single feature can save hundreds of dollars per year and significant amounts of time.

Building Ownership: Owned vs. Leased

Wilton Plaza's building at 1881 NE 26th Street, Wilton Manors, is privately owned and managed, established in 1973. This matters because your business address is tied to that physical location. If a virtual office provider leases its space and the lease ends, the provider relocates, and your business address changes with it.

Changing a business address is not just an inconvenience. It requires updating your LLC filing with the Florida Division of Corporations ($25 fee), notifying your bank, updating all licenses and permits, revising contracts, changing marketing materials, and notifying vendors and clients. For businesses with established client relationships, an address change creates confusion and raises questions about stability.

ZEN Offices leases its space. This does not mean they will relocate, but it introduces a variable that does not exist with a provider that owns its building. At Wilton Plaza, your address is permanent for as long as you are a client.

Registered Agent: Available vs. Not Offered

Every Florida LLC and corporation needs a registered agent. Wilton Plaza offers registered agent service for $99 per year at the same address, available by application (apply here). This allows clients to have their registered agent and virtual office at one location, simplifying state filings and reducing the number of service providers to manage.

ZEN Offices does not offer registered agent service. If you need a registered agent, you will need to use a separate provider, which means managing two relationships and potentially two different addresses.

Parking: Free Lot vs. Limited

Wilton Plaza has a dedicated private parking lot with private parking included for clients and visitors. When you need to pick up packages, use the conference room, or get documents notarized, you park and walk in. No meters, no garages, no searching for a spot.

Parking availability at ZEN Offices varies by location. Some locations have limited parking that may involve meters, shared lots, or nearby garages. This is worth confirming directly with ZEN Offices before committing, especially if you plan to visit the office regularly.

Mail and Package Receiving

Wilton Plaza receives mail and packages from all carriers and holds them for pickup at the office.

Ask ZEN Offices directly about its mail and package receiving terms, including any package handling fees that are not included in the base plan.

What ZEN Offices May Do Better

In the interest of a fair comparison, ZEN Offices has its own strengths. Depending on the location, ZEN Offices may offer coworking space, day offices, or dedicated desks that Wilton Plaza does not currently provide. If you need flexible workspace beyond a conference room twice per month, ZEN Offices may have options that better fit your working style.

ZEN Offices may also have multiple locations, which could be relevant if you want the flexibility of using meeting rooms or day offices in different parts of Broward County or South Florida.

The Bottom Line

If your primary needs are a professional business address, mail and package receiving, notary service, and a conference room at the lowest possible price, Wilton Plaza provides more features at a lower cost. The combination of $49/month pricing, mail and package receiving with items held for pickup, free notary, own building, private parking included, and quarterly or annual billing is unmatched by ZEN Offices or any other provider in Broward County.

If you need coworking space, day offices, or multiple locations, ZEN Offices may offer features that Wilton Plaza does not. The right choice depends on what your business actually uses.
