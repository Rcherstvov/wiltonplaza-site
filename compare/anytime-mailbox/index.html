Detailed Comparison

Anytime Mailbox is a software platform. Independent mail centers install it, set their own prices and fees, and appear in the Anytime Mailbox directory; the client gets one app across all of them. Wilton Plaza is a single owner-operated building that runs its own service. The comparison is therefore between a flat price at one known building and a low headline price at a location you have to research yourself.

What Anytime Mailbox Does Better

Entry price and flexibility. Plans start under $10 a month at many locations, there is usually no setup fee, and everything is month to month. The app handles scans, forwarding and check deposits. If you receive very little mail and only need an address on paper, the total cost can stay under $15 a month.

What Wilton Plaza Does Better

Predictability and the building. At Wilton Plaza the address is an office building with a front desk, a conference room and a notary, and the price does not move with mail volume on the Virtual Office plan. With Anytime Mailbox, the location, the staff and the fee schedule depend on the operator you pick, and the per-item charges are where a $9.99 plan becomes a $30 plan. Wilton Plaza also offers registered agent service by application, which the platform does not.

The Bottom Line

Anytime Mailbox wins on price for a light-mail address anywhere in the world. Wilton Plaza wins when the address has to look and behave like an office: for a bank, a client, a license, or a foreign founder who needs one reliable Florida location with people in it.

Comparison FAQ

Why is Anytime Mailbox so much cheaper?
The headline price covers a small number of mail items at an independent mail center. Scans, extra items and forwarding are charged separately by that operator, so real monthly cost depends on how much mail you get.
Is Anytime Mailbox one company?
It is a platform used by 2,500 independent mail centers. Wilton Plaza is one building, owner-operated, with a staffed front desk.
Can I use either address on Sunbiz?
Both provide a street address that can be used as a Florida LLC principal address once the provider has activated your service. Neither is a registered agent address by default; Wilton Plaza offers registered agent service separately by application.
