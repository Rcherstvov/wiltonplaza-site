Detailed Comparison

Davinci Virtual is one of the oldest virtual office resellers in the country. It lists more than 5,500 partner business centers and sells addresses, mail forwarding, meeting rooms and live receptionist plans on top of them, with prices given by quote. Wilton Plaza publishes its two prices and runs its own building.

What Davinci Virtual Does Better

Breadth and phone services. Davinci can put your company at an address in almost any US city and add a live receptionist or a full virtual phone system. If you need meeting rooms in several cities on one account, its booking network is a real advantage.

What Wilton Plaza Does Better

Transparency and cost. Wilton Plaza's prices are on the website: $39 or $49 a month, no setup fee, and a 12-month term that keeps the address stable on your filings. The location is a real office building with a staffed front desk, a conference room and a notary, operated by the owner rather than by a reseller and a host. Registered agent service is available by application.

The Bottom Line

Davinci fits companies that want one vendor for addresses and receptionists across many cities. Wilton Plaza fits a Florida LLC that needs one credible address at a known price, with the people who own the building answering the phone.

Comparison FAQ

Why does Davinci not publish prices?
Davinci resells partner locations and prices them individually, so it asks for a quote. Wilton Plaza publishes both plans: $39 for Business Address and $49 for Virtual Office, no setup fee.
Is a Davinci address an office building?
It is whichever partner center you choose, from executive suites to coworking floors. Wilton Plaza is a single owner-operated office building in Wilton Manors.
Which one offers registered agent service?
Wilton Plaza, at $99 per year by application under a separate agreement. Davinci does not offer it as a standard service.
