Detailed Comparison

Opus Virtual Offices is a national virtual office provider with over 650 locations across the United States, typically operating out of UPS Store or similar retail locations. Wilton Plaza is a single, owner-operated building in Wilton Manors. The two offer different feature sets at different price points.

Pricing: $588/year vs. $1,288 First Year

Wilton Plaza's Virtual Office plan costs $49 per month with no setup fee. That is $588 per year, all-in. Opus Virtual Offices charges $99 per month plus a $100 setup fee, totaling $1,288 in the first year and $1,188 each year after.

The $700 first-year savings at Wilton Plaza comes before you consider the features that are included. At Wilton Plaza, $49 per month covers complimentary mail scan requests (up to 20 per month, on written request), on-site notary at no extra charge (during business hours, subject to availability), conference room access by appointment (two sessions per month), package receiving from all carriers, junk mail filtering, a sign on the building directory, and private parking. At Opus, conference room access and notary are not included at any price.

What Opus Virtual Offices Does Better

Opus includes a live receptionist who answers calls in your company's name and transfers them to you. This is a genuine advantage for businesses that receive frequent phone calls and want a professional first impression without hiring a receptionist. Wilton Plaza does not currently offer live receptionist service.

Opus also has a national footprint with over 650 locations. If you need a business address in multiple cities, or if you want options outside of South Florida, Opus can provide addresses in markets that Wilton Plaza does not serve.

What Wilton Plaza Does Better

Price is the starting point: $50 per month less than Opus, with no setup fee and a transparent 12-month commitment billed quarterly or annually. But the feature differences go well beyond price.

Free on-site notary is included for all Virtual Office clients during business hours, subject to availability. Opus does not offer notary service at any location. For businesses that handle legal documents regularly, this eliminates the cost and inconvenience of finding a separate notary.

Building ownership is a fundamental difference. Wilton Plaza's building at 1881 NE 26th Street, Wilton Manors, is privately owned and managed, established in 1973. Opus operates out of leased spaces, typically UPS Store locations. If the store closes or the lease ends, your address changes. At Wilton Plaza, your address is permanent.

Free parking in a dedicated private lot means no searching for spots and no monthly parking fees. Conference room access for Virtual Office clients — two sessions per month, by appointment — means no hourly charges when you need to meet clients in person. Registered agent service, available by application at $99/year, keeps your registered agent and business address at the same location. Apply here.

FREC compliance matters for Florida real estate brokers. Wilton Plaza meets FREC requirements for a broker office address. Opus locations, typically in retail storefronts, do not.

The Bottom Line

If you need a live receptionist and a presence in multiple cities across the country, Opus Virtual Offices delivers on both counts. But if your needs are a professional business address, mail handling, notary, conference room, and parking in South Florida, Wilton Plaza provides significantly more features at $50/month less. The $700 first-year savings, combined with free notary, building ownership, free parking, conference room access, and quarterly or annual billing, makes Wilton Plaza the stronger value for businesses based in or serving the Broward County area.

Comparison FAQ

How much do I save with Wilton Plaza vs Opus Virtual Offices?

No. Opus Virtual Offices does not offer on-site notary service. Wilton Plaza includes a licensed on-site notary (Tricia Frost) at no extra cost for Virtual Office clients. Walk-in notary available ($10/signature).
Does Opus Virtual Offices include conference room access?
No. Conference room access is not included in the Opus Virtual Offices $99/month plan. Wilton Plaza includes conference room access for Virtual Office clients — two sessions per month, by appointment — at no additional cost.
