Detailed Analysis

Wilton Plaza and Florida Registered Agent represent two fundamentally different approaches to registered agent and virtual office service. Florida Registered Agent is an online-only provider based in St. Petersburg, focused on low-cost digital services. Wilton Plaza is a physical commercial office in Wilton Manors, Broward County, offering hands-on services backed by staff, a building, and a parking lot. This comparison examines where each provider excels and where each falls short.

Registered Agent: Same Price, Same Core Service

Both providers charge $99 per year for registered agent service. At Wilton Plaza, Registered Agent service is accepted by application and subject to eligibility review — apply here. At this price point, the core service is similar: both accept legal documents and service of process on behalf of your Florida LLC or corporation.

The difference is in the physical infrastructure behind the service. At Wilton Plaza, your registered agent address is a staffed commercial building where legal documents are accepted in person by on-site staff during business hours. Process servers deliver directly to a person who can confirm receipt. At Florida Registered Agent, the service is handled through a network that may use contracted agents or remote acceptance processes.

For most businesses, either approach works. The registered agent service is a compliance requirement, and both providers fulfill it at the same price. The decision between them comes down to what additional services you need beyond basic registered agent compliance.

Virtual Office: $49/month vs. $19/month (But Different Products)

This is where the comparison gets interesting. Florida Registered Agent offers a virtual office for $19 per month. Wilton Plaza charges $49 per month. On the surface, Florida Registered Agent appears to be the clear winner on price. But the two products are not the same thing.

Florida Registered Agent's $19/month virtual office is an online-only business-address service. There is no physical office you can visit, no on-site notary, no package receiving from UPS, FedEx, or Amazon, no conference room, no parking lot, and no sign on a building.

Wilton Plaza's $49/month Virtual Office is a full-service physical office. You receive:

  • A professional business address at a real commercial building
  • Mail receiving, held for pickup
  • On-site notary at no extra cost (Tricia Frost, licensed, during business hours, subject to availability)
  • Conference room access (2 sessions per month)
  • Package receiving from all carriers, held for pickup (USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, Amazon, couriers)
  • Mail forwarding
  • Sign hanging on the building directory
  • Private parking included
  • Walk-in service during business hours

The $30/month difference between the two services reflects the difference between an online address and a physical office with staff, notary, packages, meeting space, and parking. If you only need a mailing address, the $19/month option saves money. If you need any of the physical services listed above, the $49/month option is the better value because adding those services individually (mobile notary, conference room rental, package forwarding, etc.) would cost far more than $30/month.

Notary: On-Site vs. Not Available

Wilton Plaza has a licensed notary, Tricia Frost, available during business hours, subject to availability. Virtual Office clients receive notary service at no extra cost. Walk-in notary available ($10/signature). You can drive to 1881 NE 26th Street, park free, walk in, and get your documents notarized on the spot.

Florida Registered Agent does not offer notary service. If you need a document notarized, you will need to find a mobile notary ($25 to $75 per visit), visit a bank (if you are an account holder), or find a UPS Store or other notary location. For businesses that notarize documents regularly, the absence of notary service is a significant gap.

Package Receiving: All Carriers vs. Mail Only

Modern businesses receive packages from multiple carriers. Amazon deliveries, vendor shipments, returns, and supplies arrive via UPS, FedEx, DHL, Amazon's own delivery network, and private couriers. A service that only handles USPS mail misses a significant portion of business deliveries.

Wilton Plaza receives packages from all carriers and holds them for pickup. Pick up in person (private parking included) or request forwarding. There are no per-package fees.

Florida Registered Agent's online-only model does not include package receiving. If your business receives packages, you will need a separate solution.

Conference Room: Available vs. Not Available

The Virtual Office at Wilton Plaza plan includes two conference room reservations per month at no additional charge. The room is professional, private, and suitable for client meetings, team sessions, multi-party signings, and interviews. When you need to meet someone face to face, you have a professional space to do it.

Florida Registered Agent does not have conference rooms because they do not have a physical office that clients visit. If you need meeting space, you will need to book it separately through a coworking space or meeting room rental service, typically at $25 to $75 per hour.

Location: Broward County vs. Pinellas County

Wilton Plaza is located in Wilton Manors, Broward County, in the heart of the South Florida tri-county area. If your business operates in Southeast Florida, your address is in the right geographic market.

Florida Registered Agent is based in St. Petersburg, Pinellas County, on Florida's Gulf Coast. For registered agent purposes, the physical location of the RA address does not need to be in the same county as your business operations. However, for a virtual office address that appears on business cards, invoices, and marketing materials, the geographic location matters. A St. Petersburg address may not make sense for a business that operates primarily in South Florida.

What Florida Registered Agent Does Better

In fairness, Florida Registered Agent has genuine strengths:

  • Lower virtual office price. At $19/month, their virtual office is $30/month cheaper than Wilton Plaza. For businesses that only need a mailing address without physical services, this is a real savings.
  • LLC formation service. Florida Registered Agent offers LLC formation for $209 (plus state filing fees). If you are forming a new Florida LLC and want to bundle registered agent and formation, this is convenient. Wilton Plaza does not currently offer LLC formation.
  • Online-first experience. If you never intend to visit a physical office, Florida Registered Agent's fully digital workflow may be more convenient than an office-based service.

What Wilton Plaza Does Better

  • Physical presence. A real building with staff, not an online-only service. Process servers, delivery drivers, and clients can physically interact with a staffed office.
  • On-site notary. Free for Virtual Office clients. Not available at all from Florida Registered Agent.
  • Mail and package receiving. Mail and packages from all carriers are received and held for pickup.
  • Conference room. Professional meeting space twice per month, included.
  • Building ownership. Privately owned building, established 1973. Permanent address with no lease risk.
  • Private parking included. Dedicated lot for clients and visitors.
  • Broward County location. If your business operates in Southeast Florida, your address is in the right market.
  • Bilingual staff. English and Spanish.

Which One Is Right for You?

Choose Florida Registered Agent if all you need is a standalone registered agent ($99/year, same price as our add-on) and a basic virtual mailing address ($19/month), you never need to visit a physical office, you do not need notary service, package receiving, or a conference room, and you are comfortable with an online-only provider based on the Gulf Coast.

Choose Wilton Plaza if you want a physical office with staff that you can visit, you need on-site notary service (free with VO plan), your business receives packages from multiple carriers, you occasionally need conference room space for meetings, you want a Broward County address for a Southeast Florida business, and you value a permanent address in a building the provider owns.

Both providers offer the same registered agent service at the same price. The difference is in the virtual office and the physical services that come with it. Visit our pricing page for full details, or call (954) 249-5388.
