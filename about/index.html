Our Story

Wilton Plaza is a real commercial building at 1881 NE 26th Street in Wilton Manors. Privately owned and managed. We are not a franchise, not a coworking chain, and not a middleman leasing space from someone else.

That matters because your business address is only as stable as the building behind it. Landlords change. Leases expire. Chains close locations. Our building stays in our hands, and your address stays the same.

We started with property management and added virtual office services because tenants and local businesses kept asking for the same things: a professional address, reliable mail handling, notary services, and someone who actually picks up the phone. Wilton Plaza is owner-operated by MEGA MGMT.

Located in Wilton Manors, Broward County — in the heart of South Florida, 30 minutes from downtown Miami and 15 minutes from Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport.
