What FREC Requires

Florida Statute 475.22 and Rule 61J2-10.022 F.A.C.

1. Registered Office — A physical address registered with DBPR as the brokerage office of record.

2. Physical Sign — Brokerage trade name and "Licensed Real Estate Broker" displayed at the office entrance or building directory.

3. Entrance to Public — The office must be accessible to the public during business hours.

4. Current License Displayed — The broker's license must be visible at the office location.

5. Office Must Be Staffed — Someone must be present at the office during advertised business hours.
